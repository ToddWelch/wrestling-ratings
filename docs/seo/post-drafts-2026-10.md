- **URL slug:** /reports/wrestling-tv-ratings-october-2026

Three weeks into October, the cable wrestling shows are running ahead of where
they were a year ago.

**SmackDown** has averaged **1.31 million** viewers over its first three October
episodes (Oct 2, 9 and 16), with a 0.30 rating in the 18-49 key demo. Last
October it averaged 1.06 million. The Oct 9 episode drew 1.35 million, its best

**AEW Dynamite** is averaging **624,000** in October against 451,000 last October.

October 2025. The Oct 13 episode reached 732,000, NXT's third-best audience of
2026.

Last October it averaged 314,000 across two airings.

**TNA iMPACT** has held between **182,000 and 252,000** this month on AMC, its usual
range. TNA was not on AMC a year ago, so there is no October comparison yet.

**WWE Raw on Netflix** has settled at **2.4 to 2.5 million** global views per week,

## 2. SmackDown viewership in 2026

- **Title:** SmackDown Viewership 2026: Down 14%, With Context
- **Meta description:** SmackDown averaged 1.24 million viewers in 2026 through October, down 14% on 2025. Here is how much of that lines up with Nielsen's 2025 change.
- **Target search phrase:** smackdown viewership 2026
- **URL slug:** /reports/smackdown-viewership-2026

SmackDown has averaged **1.24 million** viewers across its 42 episodes from Jan 2
to Oct 16, 2026. Over the same stretch of 2025 it averaged **1.44 million**. That
is a drop of about **14 percent**, and the 18-49 demo fell further, from 0.41 to
0.29.

The headline number hides a measurement change. Nielsen switched to its Big Data
+ Panel method on Sept 26, 2025. In the eight episodes before that date,
SmackDown averaged 1.40 million. In the eight after, it averaged 1.11 million.
Football season starts the same month, so the change is not the only factor,
but most of 2025's comparison weeks were measured the old way and every 2026
week is measured the new way.

The fairer comparison is October against October, since both sit on the new
method. SmackDown is averaging **1.31 million** this October against 1.06 million
last October, an increase of about **23 percent**.

Inside 2026, April was the high point: the show averaged 1.44 million that
month, and the April 17 episode drew **1.58 million** with a 0.42 demo, the best
of the year. The low was Jan 23 at 943,000. Two February episodes aired on SyFy
(Feb 13 and Feb 20) and drew 1.04 and 1.10 million.

The weekly chart shows the full run back to January 2025, with the measurement
change marked.

**Fact-check before publishing:** refresh the averages; keep the "football season"
caveat; confirm the 2026-27 season methodology question.

## 3. AEW Dynamite ratings in 2026

- **Title:** AEW Dynamite Ratings in 2026 Are Up 8% on Last Year
- **Meta description:** Dynamite averaged 657,000 viewers in 2026 through October 21, up 8% on the same weeks of 2025, despite a measurement change that cut its numbers.
- **Target search phrase:** aew dynamite ratings 2026
- **URL slug:** /reports/aew-dynamite-ratings-2026

AEW Dynamite has averaged **657,000** viewers across 42 episodes from Jan 7 to
Oct 21, 2026. The same weeks of 2025 averaged **608,000**, so the show is up about
**8 percent** year over year.

That gain came against a headwind. When Nielsen moved to Big Data + Panel on
Sept 26, 2025, Dynamite's average fell from 606,000 in the eight weeks before to
480,000 in the eight weeks after. Most of the 2025 weeks in this comparison
were measured the old way, which flattered 2025. Dynamite still finished ahead.

The 18-49 demo tells a different story: 0.12 in 2026 against 0.16 in 2025.

The year has trended up, with a soft patch in May and June. January averaged
548,000. By July the monthly average was 715,000, and September was the best
month at **726,000**. The Sept 30
episode drew **899,000** and a 0.22 demo, the high for 2026. The low came the
following week, Oct 6, at 495,000.

October is averaging 624,000 so far, up from 451,000 last October.

Track Dynamite against Collision, NXT and SmackDown week by week on the chart.

and Oct 6 before naming either episode; confirm the 2026-27 season methodology

## 4. TNA iMPACT on AMC

- **Title:** TNA iMPACT on AMC: Ten Months of Ratings
- **Meta description:** TNA iMPACT has averaged 217,000 viewers across 37 episodes on AMC in 2026. The best week, the weakest week, and how the year has trended.
- **Target search phrase:** tna impact ratings amc
- **URL slug:** /reports/tna-impact-ratings-amc-2026

TNA iMPACT moved to AMC in January 2026. Through Oct 15, it has aired **37
episodes** there and averaged **217,000** viewers with a 0.04 rating in the 18-49
demo.

The show started at 173,000 on Jan 15, then climbed through the winter. February
and March averaged 240,000 and 254,000. Spring was the soft patch: May averaged
183,000, and May 7 was the lowest week of the year at **164,000**.

Summer brought it back. July averaged 256,000, the best month so far, and the
July 23 episode drew **307,000**, the only week above 300,000. Since then the show
has held between 176,000 and 252,000; the Oct 15 episode drew 252,000.

For scale, AEW Collision averaged 405,000 over the same months, and Dynamite
657,000. TNA's audience is smaller, but it has been steady, and it has not had a
week under 160,000.

Every TNA week on AMC is on the chart alongside the WWE and AEW shows.

## 5. WWE Raw on Netflix, year two

- **Title:** WWE Raw on Netflix Viewership: 2026 vs 2025
- **Meta description:** Raw averaged 2.66 million global Netflix views a week in 2026 through October, down 7% on the same weeks of 2025. The highs, lows and trend.
- **Target search phrase:** wwe raw netflix viewership
- **URL slug:** /reports/wwe-raw-netflix-viewership-2026

WWE Raw has averaged **2.66 million** global views per week on Netflix from Jan 5
to Oct 19, 2026. Over the same weeks of 2025, its first year on Netflix, it
averaged **2.86 million**. That is a drop of about **7 percent**.

Netflix counts a "view" as total hours watched divided by the episode's runtime.
It is a global number, so it does not line up with the Nielsen numbers for
SmackDown, NXT or AEW, which measure US viewers.

The spring was strong. March and April averaged 2.94 and 2.98 million, and the
April 20 episode reached **3.3 million**, the best week of 2026. Summer was the
low point: June averaged 2.28 million, and June 1 drew **2.0 million**, the
weakest week of the year.

Since August, Raw has mostly held between 2.4 and 2.7 million, with one jump to
3.1 million on Sept 14. October is averaging
2.47 million, nearly identical to October 2025 (2.43 million). Netflix did not
publish numbers for two January weeks (Jan 12 and Jan 26), so those weeks are
missing from both the average and the chart.

See every Raw week on the streaming chart.

before naming it.
